# Zuna Facecap

**Use your Android phone as a face tracker for Warudo, VSeeFace and VNyan. No iPhone needed.**

Your PC app sees the phone as iFacialMocap. Camera video stays on the phone; only tracking numbers go to your PC over your own Wi-Fi.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/ZunaTtang/zuna-facecap-releases/releases/latest) on your phone and tap the **`ZunaFacecap-<version>.apk`** file under **Assets**.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager (a one-time setting), then install.
3. Open **Zuna Facecap Dev**. The first-run guide starts on its own.

Requirements: Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit (ARM64) phone, 4 GB RAM or more, and the phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi. → [Device requirements](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#1-device-requirements)

- The APK installs as **Zuna Facecap Dev** and can sit next to the Google Play version. Use one of them at a time.
- New versions show up inside the app (Settings → Updates). The `release.json` file next to each APK is what the app reads; you don't need to download it.

## Set up in 3 steps

1. **Stand the phone up** 30–50 cm from your face, at eye level, lit from the front. → [Placing the phone](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#placing-the-phone)
2. **Connect your PC app**: choose the iFacialMocap tracker and enter the phone address shown in the app. → [Warudo](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#warudo) · [VSeeFace](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#vseeface) · [VNyan](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#vnyan)
3. **Tap Start tracking and calibrate once** (45 s; a 4 s quick calibration after you move the phone). → [Calibrate](https://zunattang.github.io/zuna-facecap-releases/guide/#5-calibrate)
